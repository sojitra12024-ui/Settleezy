# First 30 Days in Berlin: Checklist for International Students (2026)

*Last updated: October 2026 · By the Settleezy Team*

**Quick answer:** In your first 30 days in Berlin: (1) get a **German SIM**, (2) move in and get your **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung**, (3) do your **Anmeldung within 14 days**, (4) **activate your blocked account** and open a **current account**, (5) confirm your **health insurance**, (6) **enrol** at your university and activate your **semester ticket**, and (7) book your **residence permit** appointment before your entry visa expires.

## Before you arrive

- [ ] Admission letter and visa
- [ ] Blocked account (€11,904 for 2026) and travel/incoming health insurance
- [ ] Accommodation booked (at least temporary), with a registrable address
- [ ] Copies of key documents (passport, admission, insurance, certificates)
- [ ] Some euros in cash and a card that works abroad

## Week 1

- [ ] **Get a German SIM card**: prepaid is the fastest option ([guide](/guides/sim-card-berlin-students/))
- [ ] **Move in** and get the signed **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung**
- [ ] **Book your Anmeldung** appointment ([guide](/guides/anmeldung-berlin-students/))
- [ ] Buy a transport ticket until your semester ticket is active ([guide](/guides/public-transport-berlin-students/))

## Week 2

- [ ] **Do your Anmeldung** (deadline: 14 days after moving in) → get your Meldebescheinigung
- [ ] **Activate your blocked account** and open a free **current account**
- [ ] **Confirm health insurance** with a public insurer, or arrange private insurance ([guide](/guides/health-insurance-students-germany/))

## Week 3

- [ ] **Enrol (immatrikulieren)** at your university and pay the semester contribution
- [ ] **Activate your semester ticket**
- [ ] Register for the **Rundfunkbeitrag** if your flat isn't registered yet (€18.36 per flat per month)

## Week 4

- [ ] **Book your residence permit** appointment at the Landesamt für Einwanderung (LEA) well before your visa expires
- [ ] Set your **monthly budget** ([cost of living guide](/guides/cost-of-living-berlin-students/))
- [ ] Find a **Mensa**, a **discount supermarket** and a **used bike**
- [ ] Meet people: university welcome events and student communities

## FAQ

### What should I do first when I arrive in Berlin as a student?
Get a German SIM card, move in, get your landlord confirmation (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung), and book your Anmeldung appointment so you can register within 14 days.

### When do I need to apply for a residence permit in Berlin?
Before your entry visa expires, at the Landesamt für Einwanderung (LEA) Berlin. Appointments can take time, so book early.

### Can I open a bank account before Anmeldung?
Some online banks and blocked account providers let you open an account before registration. Many traditional banks require your Meldebescheinigung.
