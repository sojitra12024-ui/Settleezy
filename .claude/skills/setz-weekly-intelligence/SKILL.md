# Weekly intelligence

Run these agents in parallel and give each only what it needs:
- `partner-manager`: movement by stage this week, stuck deals, partners at risk.
- `growth-analyst`: the numbers against goals, what moved and why.
- `competitor-intelligence`: what changed at Groupon, NeoTaste, vspots, UNiDAYS, Student Beans, ISIC.
- `social-intelligence`: best and weakest posts, audience change, three content ideas.
- `student-outreach`: university pipeline and the intake calendar.
- `platform-operations`: site and form health.

## Output
1. **The week in three sentences.**
2. **Scorecard**: a small table, this week vs last week vs goal. Only numbers with a source.
3. **Wins, problems, surprises.**
4. **Recommended focus for this week**: three things, with the reason and the first action for each.
5. **Decisions needed from you.** If one is strategic, offer to convene the council.

Save to `Setz/Log/briefings/YYYY-Www-weekly.md` and update the knowledge files the agents flagged.
