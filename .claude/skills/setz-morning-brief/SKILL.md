# Morning brief

Gather in parallel, then write one brief. Read-only except for the files noted at the end.

1. **Schedule** (`executive-assistant`): today's events from the Outlook calendar with time, who, and one line of
   context from the last thread or meeting note. Flag clashes and meetings that need preparation.
2. **Inbox** (`inbox-manager`): emails received since the last brief that need a reply, most important first;
   threads where he owes a reply for more than two days; replies from partners or universities.
3. **Follow-ups** (`partner-manager`): what is due today from `Setz/Log/followups.md` and the outreach tracker
   report; agreements sent and unsigned; forms promised and not received.
4. **Deadlines and tasks**: due or overdue items in `Setz/Log/tasks.md`.
5. **Settleezy updates**: any change worth knowing in `Setz/Settleezy/growth.md` or from the latest routine
   reports. Skip this section when nothing changed.
6. **Radar**: run the `setz-radar` checks and include what crosses a threshold.
7. **Operational issues**: anything broken or at risk (failed routine, bounced emails, a connector that is down).

## Output (short enough to read on the phone)
- **Top five priorities today**, each one line with the reason it is on the list.
- **Today**: the schedule.
- **Needs your reply** (max 7), **Follow-ups due** (max 7), **Deadlines**.
- **Heads-up**: issues, or "nothing unusual".
- **Prepared for you**: drafts already waiting in Outlook Drafts, each marked PREPARED.

Then save the brief to `Setz/Log/briefings/YYYY-MM-DD-morning.md`.
In a scheduled run nobody is present: do only autonomous work. Draft replies if useful, never send.
If a source cannot be reached, say which one in the brief instead of leaving a silent gap.
