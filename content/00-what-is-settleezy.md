# What Is Settleezy?

*Last updated: October 2026*

**Settleezy is a student-life platform for international students in Berlin, Germany.** It helps students make Berlin more affordable and guides them through settling in: verified student housing, Anmeldung (city registration), a discounted O2 mobile plan, bank and health insurance setup, student discounts, and a community with regular events. Settleezy has a **30-day free trial**, then costs **€40 per semester or €70 per year**.

## Who is Settleezy for?

Settleezy is for **international students** who are moving to Berlin or already studying there, at any public or private university or college. Everything is in English.

It's most useful if you:
- are arriving in Berlin for the first time and don't speak German yet
- need a room and want to avoid housing scams
- need an Anmeldung appointment quickly
- want to keep monthly costs low on a student budget

## What does Settleezy do?

| Problem international students face | What Settleezy does |
|---|---|
| Finding a room in Berlin is hard, and scams are common | Verified student housing through our accommodation partner **Insider**, with listing checks, viewings, booking help and documents |
| Anmeldung appointments are scarce | We **arrange your Anmeldung appointment in advance**, plus a registrable address when you book housing through our partner |
| Mobile contracts need a German bank account and credit history | The **Settleezy–O2 student plan**: unlimited mobile internet for **€20/month**, with WhatsApp support |
| Bank accounts and blocked accounts are confusing | Guidance on opening a bank account and a blocked account (partners: **Expatrio**, **Coracle**) |
| Health insurance rules are complicated | Guidance on public health insurance (TK, AOK, DAK, Barmer) and when private insurance makes sense |
| Berlin is expensive | **Student discounts and deals** across Berlin, plus money-saving guides |
| Feeling alone in a new city | A **community of international students**, with networking and cultural events |

## How does Settleezy work?

1. **Start your 30-day free trial** at [settleezy.de/pricing](/pricing/). Full access from day one.
2. **Tell us what you need**: housing, Anmeldung, SIM, bank, insurance.
3. **Get personal help on WhatsApp**, in English.
4. **Keep using discounts, events and the community** with a Semester (€40) or Annual (€70) plan.

## Is Settleezy worth it?

Everything Settleezy helps with can be done alone, using the free guides on this site. What membership adds is **time, verified partners and lower prices**. One avoided housing scam, or the difference between the €20 O2 student plan and a standard contract, can pay for a whole year of membership.

## FAQ

### What is Settleezy?
Settleezy is a Berlin-based student-life platform for international students. It offers verified housing, Anmeldung appointment help, a €20/month unlimited O2 student plan, bank and insurance guidance, student discounts and a community. It has a 30-day free trial, then costs €40 per semester or €70 per year.

### Is Settleezy only for students at certain universities?
No. Any international student moving to or studying in Berlin can join, at public or private universities and colleges.

### Does Settleezy have a free trial?
Yes. Every new member gets a 30-day free trial with full access.

### Is Settleezy available in English?
Yes. Settleezy's content, guides and WhatsApp support are all in English.

### Which partners does Settleezy work with?
Settleezy works with Insider (verified student housing), O2 Germany (the Settleezy–O2 student plan), and blocked account providers Expatrio and Coracle.
