# Cost of Living in Berlin for International Students (2026)

*Last updated: October 2026 · By the Settleezy Team*

**Quick answer:** An international student in Berlin spends about **€1,100–€1,350 per month** in 2026 when renting a room in a shared flat, or about **€850–€950** in a student dorm. Rent is the biggest cost: an average 20 m² WG room costs **€659/month** (MLP Student Housing Report 2026). Add health insurance (about **€146**), transport (**€37.80** with the semester ticket) and food (**€200–€300**).

## Is the blocked account amount enough for Berlin?

The blocked account lets you withdraw **€992 per month**. That covers a frugal budget (dorm or a room under about €450), but **not** a typical one with an average €659 WG room. Most students close the gap with a part-time job. Non-EU students can work 140 full days or 280 half days per year at a minimum wage of €13.90/hour in 2026. See [student jobs in Berlin](/guides/student-jobs-berlin/).

## How Berlin compares with other German cities

Berlin is the **second most expensive** German university city for student housing, after Munich, according to the MLP Student Housing Report 2026. Food, transport and insurance cost about the same everywhere in Germany, so rent is what makes the difference.

## FAQ

### How much does it cost to live in Berlin as a student per month?
About €1,100–€1,350 per month with a shared-flat room, or €850–€950 in a student dorm (2026).

### How much is rent for students in Berlin?
An average 20 m² room in a shared flat costs €659 per month (MLP Student Housing Report 2026). Studierendenwerk dorm rooms are cheaper but have waiting lists.

### How much is health insurance for students in Germany?
Public student health insurance costs about €140–€150 per month in 2026, depending on the insurer's additional contribution and your age. At TK it's €146.29 for students aged 23+ without children.

### Do international students pay tuition in Berlin?
Public universities in Berlin generally don't charge tuition, only a semester contribution of roughly €300–€350 that includes the semester ticket. Private universities charge tuition.
