# Pricing — Settleezy

Settleezy is a membership platform for international students in Berlin. Prices are in euros (EUR) and include VAT.
Last updated: October 2026

## Free trial
- Price: €0 for 30 days
- Includes: full access to Settleezy member features
- After the trial: choose the Semester or Annual plan

## Semester plan
- Price: €40 per semester (one payment)
- Duration: one semester (about 6 months)
- Effective cost: about €6.70 per month

## Annual plan
- Price: €70 per year (one payment)
- Duration: 12 months (two semesters)
- Effective cost: about €5.85 per month
- Saves €10 compared with two Semester plans

## Included in every plan (and in the free trial)
- Verified student housing support through Settleezy's accommodation partner Insider (listing verification, viewings, booking and documents)
- Anmeldung (city registration): appointment arranged in advance, plus a registrable address when you book housing through the partner
- Settleezy–O2 student plan: unlimited mobile internet for €20/month, with WhatsApp support from a Settleezy O2 representative
- Bank account and blocked account (Sperrkonto) guidance, with partners Expatrio and Coracle
- Health insurance guidance (public insurers such as TK, AOK, DAK, Barmer)
- Berlin student discounts and deals
- Community: events, networking and the verified student community
- English-language support via WhatsApp

## Who can join
- International students moving to Berlin, or already studying in Berlin, at any university or college (public or private)
