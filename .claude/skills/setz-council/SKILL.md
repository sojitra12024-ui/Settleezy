# The SETZ Council

The council exists to improve a decision, not to agree with it. Ten answers that agree are a failure.

## Before you convene
1. Restate the decision as one question with the options on the table. If it is vague, ask Mruddual one
   clarifying question first.
2. Read the relevant knowledge files in `Setz/Settleezy/` (and `Setz/Log/decision-register.md` for earlier decisions on
   the same topic). Write a one-page **brief**: the question, the facts with their dates, what is unknown, the
   constraints (money, time, team). Every member gets the same brief and nothing else from this conversation.

## Round 1: independent positions
Run the ten members in `personas.md` as **separate subagents in parallel**, each with only the brief and its own
persona. Members must not see each other's answers in this round. Each may research (web, knowledge files) and
returns, in under 200 words:
- **Position**: one clear recommendation, with a confidence from 1 to 5.
- **The two reasons** that matter most, with evidence.
- **What would change my mind.**
- **The risk the others will miss.**

If subagents are not available (for example in a chat on the phone), write the ten positions one at a time, and
finish each before starting the next so later ones do not echo earlier ones.

## Round 2: debate
1. Group the positions into camps. Name the real disagreements (not wording differences).
2. For each disagreement, give the strongest member of each camp the other camp's argument and ask for a direct
   rebuttal or a concession, in under 120 words. The Critical Thinking Advisor attacks the majority position
   whatever it is.
3. Record who changed their mind and why. A member may hold out; do not force consensus.

## Round 3: consolidated recommendation (you, as Setz)
Give Mruddual:
1. **Recommendation** in two sentences, with your confidence.
2. **Why**: the three arguments that survived the debate.
3. **Dissent**: who still disagrees and their best argument, stated fairly.
4. **Risks and how to limit them.** Anything legal, financial or irreversible is flagged by name.
5. **What we don't know** and the cheapest way to find out.
6. **Next steps**, each tagged `[autonomous]`, `[approval]` or `[explicit]`.
7. A table: member, position, confidence, changed mind (yes/no).

Then write the decision (once Mruddual makes it) to `Setz/Log/decision-register.md` using the entry format in
`setz-decision` (ID, alternatives, council contributions with dissent, reasoning, evidence, review date).

## Rules
- Members argue from their role's incentives. They use only facts from the brief or sources they cite.
- No member may say "it depends" without saying on what, and which way they lean.
- The council advises. Mruddual decides. Nothing is executed by the council.
