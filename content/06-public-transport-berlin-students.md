# Public Transport in Berlin for Students: Semesterticket vs Deutschlandticket (2026)

*Last updated: October 2026 · By the Settleezy Team*

**Quick answer:** If your university includes it, the **Deutschland-Semesterticket** is the cheapest option. It costs **€226.80 per semester (€37.80/month)** from winter semester 2026/27 and is valid on all local and regional transport in Germany. If your university doesn't offer one (common at private universities), buy the **Deutschlandticket for €63/month**. Riding without a valid ticket costs a **€60 fine**.

## Your options compared

| Ticket | Price (2026) | Where it's valid | Who can get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deutschland-Semesterticket | €226.80/semester (≈ €37.80/month) from WiSe 2026/27 | All local and regional transport in Germany | Students at participating universities (paid with the semester fee) |
| Deutschlandticket | €63/month (monthly subscription, cancel monthly) | All local and regional transport in Germany | Anyone |
| Single tickets | Several euros per ride | Berlin zones AB or ABC | Anyone |
| Bike | One-time cost, often €80–€200 used | Anywhere | Anyone |

The Deutschland-Semesterticket is priced at **60% of the Deutschlandticket**. It stayed at €34.80/month up to summer semester 2026 and rose to €37.80 from winter semester 2026/27.

## Does my university include the semester ticket?

- **Most public universities in Berlin** (such as FU, HU, TU, UdK, HTW, HWR, BHT) include the Deutschland-Semesterticket in the semester contribution. Check your university's student union (AStA) page.
- **Many private universities** don't include a semester ticket. Their students usually buy the regular Deutschlandticket.

## What the ticket covers

- U-Bahn, S-Bahn, trams, buses and ferries in Berlin
- Regional trains (RE, RB) across Germany, so weekend trips to Potsdam, Leipzig, Dresden, Hamburg or the Baltic Sea cost nothing extra
- **Not covered:** long-distance trains (ICE, IC, EC) and FlixTrain/FlixBus

## Money-saving tips

1. **Never ride without a ticket.** Controls are common, and the fine (erhöhtes Beförderungsentgelt) is €60.
2. **Have your ticket ready on your phone** in your university or BVG app before boarding.
3. **Get a used bike.** Berlin is flat and has many bike lanes. Use a strong lock; bike theft is common.
4. **Plan long trips early.** Deutsche Bahn's saver fares and the BahnCard 25 for young people can make ICE trips much cheaper.

## FAQ

### How much is the semester ticket in Berlin in 2026?
From winter semester 2026/27, the Deutschland-Semesterticket costs €226.80 per semester (€37.80/month). It's paid with your semester contribution at participating universities.

### Is the Deutschlandticket cheaper for students?
Students at participating universities get the Deutschland-Semesterticket at 60% of the regular price. Others pay the regular Deutschlandticket price of €63/month in 2026.

### Can I use the semester ticket outside Berlin?
Yes. The Deutschland-Semesterticket is valid on all local and regional public transport in Germany, but not on long-distance trains like ICE or IC.

### What is the fine for riding without a ticket in Berlin?
€60.
