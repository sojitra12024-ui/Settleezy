## What you need to activate a SIM in Germany

- **Passport** (or EU ID card)
- **Identity verification:** by video call (VideoIdent), at a shop, or at a post office (PostIdent). German law requires it for every SIM.
- **For contracts:** usually a German bank account (IBAN) and a registered address

## Tips to save on your phone bill

1. **Buy a prepaid SIM in your first week**, so you have a German number for flat viewings, banks and appointments.
2. **Avoid 24-month contracts** until you know your budget.
3. **Use Wi-Fi at university** (eduroam) and at home.
4. **Check roaming:** EU roaming is included in most German plans, which is handy for trips.

## FAQ

### What is the cheapest SIM card for students in Berlin?
Prepaid SIMs from discount providers start at about €10/month and don't need a German bank account. For unlimited data, the Settleezy–O2 student plan costs €20/month.

### Can I get a German SIM card without Anmeldung?
Usually yes for prepaid SIMs: you only need your passport and ID verification. Contracts often require a registered address and a German bank account.

### Do I need a German bank account for a phone contract?
Most contracts require a German or SEPA bank account for monthly direct debit. Prepaid SIMs can be topped up without one.
