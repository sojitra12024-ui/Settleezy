## FAQ

### How much does Settleezy cost?
Settleezy costs €40 per semester or €70 per year after a 30-day free trial.

### What is included in the Settleezy free trial?
The 30-day free trial gives full access to Settleezy member features: housing support, Anmeldung help, the O2 student plan, bank and insurance guidance, discounts, events and WhatsApp support.

### What's the difference between the Semester and Annual plans?
Both include the same features. The Semester plan (€40) covers one semester. The Annual plan (€70) covers 12 months and saves €10 compared with paying for two semesters.

### Who can sign up for Settleezy?
Any international student moving to or studying in Berlin, at any university or college.
