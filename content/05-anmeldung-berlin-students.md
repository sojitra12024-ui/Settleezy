# Anmeldung in Berlin for International Students: Step-by-Step (2026)

*Last updated: October 2026 · By the Settleezy Team*

**Quick answer:** Anmeldung is the mandatory registration of your address in Germany. You must do it **within 14 days of moving in**, at any **Bürgeramt** in Berlin. It's **free**. Bring your **passport**, the completed **registration form (Anmeldeformular)** and the **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung** (landlord confirmation). You get a **Meldebescheinigung** (registration certificate) on the spot, and your **tax ID** arrives by post a few weeks later.

## Why Anmeldung matters for students

You need your registration certificate (Meldebescheinigung) to:
- apply for or extend your **residence permit**
- receive your **tax ID (Steuer-ID)**, which you need for any job
- open many **bank accounts**
- sign some **phone and internet contracts**

## Documents you need

| Document | Notes |
|---|---|
| Passport (or national ID for EU citizens) | Original |
| Anmeldeformular (registration form) | Download from service.berlin.de, fill in German |
| Wohnungsgeberbestätigung | Signed by your landlord or main tenant |
| Visa (if applicable) | In your passport |
| Marriage/birth certificates | Only when registering family members |

## Step-by-step Anmeldung in Berlin

1. **Move into your room** and get the signed Wohnungsgeberbestätigung from your landlord.
2. **Book an appointment** at service.berlin.de under "Anmeldung einer Wohnung". You can use **any Bürgeramt in Berlin**, not just the one in your district.
3. **Check often for free slots.** Appointments are scarce. New slots are often released early in the morning, and cancellations appear at short notice.
4. **Fill in the Anmeldeformular** before your appointment.
5. **Go to the appointment** with all documents. It usually takes 10–15 minutes.
6. **Get your Meldebescheinigung** and keep it safe; you'll need it often.
7. **Wait for your tax ID letter**, which normally arrives by post within a few weeks. Make sure your name is on your mailbox.

## Common problems

- **No appointment within 14 days:** book the earliest available slot and keep proof of your booking attempts. Bürgerämter know appointments are scarce.
- **Landlord won't give a Wohnungsgeberbestätigung:** landlords are legally required to provide one (§ 19 Bundesmeldegesetz). If a landlord refuses, that's a strong warning sign about the rental.
- **Moving again:** you must re-register within 14 days at your new address.
- **Leaving Germany:** do an Abmeldung (deregistration) before you leave.

## FAQ

### How long do I have to do my Anmeldung in Berlin?
Within 14 days of moving into your accommodation.

### Is Anmeldung free in Berlin?
Yes. Registering your address is free of charge.

### Can I do my Anmeldung at any Bürgeramt in Berlin?
Yes. You can register at any Bürgeramt in Berlin, regardless of which district you live in.

### What is a Wohnungsgeberbestätigung?
A form your landlord or main tenant signs to confirm you moved into the property. You need it for Anmeldung.

### When will I get my tax ID after Anmeldung?
The Federal Central Tax Office usually posts your tax ID within a few weeks of registration.
