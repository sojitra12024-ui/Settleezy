## What Settleezy is
Settleezy helps **students in Berlin**, especially **international students**, save money and settle in. It has three parts:

1. **The Settleezy membership and app**, for students who are already in Berlin and for those who are new:
   - **Savings:** members save money at partner **restaurants, cafés, grocery stores, activities, events and more**. The app shows the venues where they can save.
   - **Guides** to life in Berlin
   - **Expense and savings tracker**
   - **Document vault** for important documents
   - **Emergency numbers**
2. **Workshops** organised by Settleezy, for example building a **portfolio**, writing a **CV**, and more.
3. **The Buddy platform for universities:** Settleezy partners with universities in Berlin to support their **new incoming students** with **A-to-Z support**, including **accommodation support**.

## Who it's for
- Students in Berlin at any university or college
- Especially international students: new to the city, on a tight budget, and not yet sure where the good deals are or how things work

## Why students join
- Berlin is expensive on a student budget; regular savings on food, groceries and going out add up quickly
- One app for savings, guides, budgeting, documents and emergency numbers
- The 30-day free trial means students can try it before paying

## Why partners join
- **Venues** (restaurants, cafés, grocery stores, activities, event organisers): a steady stream of new, local, repeat customers. Students live in Berlin for years, unlike one-time tourists, and international students are actively looking for new regular spots when they arrive.
- **Universities:** the Buddy platform gives their incoming students A-to-Z support, including accommodation support, from day one.
