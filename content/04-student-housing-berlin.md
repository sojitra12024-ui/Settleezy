# How to Find Student Housing in Berlin Without Getting Scammed (2026)

*Last updated: October 2026 · By the Settleezy Team*

**Quick answer:** Start searching **2–3 months before you arrive**. Apply for a **Studierendenwerk Berlin dorm** right after admission, and search shared flats (WG) on WG-Gesucht and verified platforms at the same time. An average WG room costs **€659/month** in 2026. **Never send money before a viewing and a signed contract**, and make sure the landlord will give you a **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung**, the landlord confirmation you need for Anmeldung.

## Types of student housing in Berlin

| Type | Typical monthly rent | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studierendenwerk dorm | Usually well below market | Cheapest, furnished, registrable | Long waiting lists |
| WG room (shared flat) | ≈ €659 average | Split costs, social | High competition, scams |
| Private student residence | Often €650–€950+ | Easy to book from abroad | Expensive |
| Temporary furnished room | Often €700+ | Good for the first months | Expensive, short contracts |
| Own studio | ≈ €725 (30 m²) | Privacy | Hard to get without German income |

*WG and studio figures: MLP Studentenwohnreport 2026.*

## Step-by-step: finding a room in Berlin

1. **Apply for a dorm immediately.** Register with Studierendenwerk Berlin as soon as you have your admission letter.
2. **Set your budget.** Keep warm rent (rent including utilities) at or below about 40–45% of your monthly money.
3. **Search several channels.** WG-Gesucht, Studierendenwerk Berlin, university housing boards, student residences and verified partners.
4. **Write short, personal messages.** Mention your university, programme, move-in date and a little about yourself. In WGs, flatmates choose who moves in.
5. **Do a viewing**, in person or by live video call where the person shows the actual room and building.
6. **Check the contract.** Rent, utilities (Nebenkosten), deposit, start and end date, and whether you can register (Anmeldung) at the address.
7. **Pay the deposit only after signing,** by bank transfer to a German or EU account in the landlord's name, never by Western Union, crypto or gift cards.
8. **Get the Wohnungsgeberbestätigung** when you move in, so you can register within 14 days.

## 8 red flags of Berlin housing scams

1. The rent is far below the market rate for that area.
2. The landlord is "abroad" and can't show the flat.
3. You're asked to pay a deposit or "reservation fee" before a viewing.
4. Payment by Western Union, MoneyGram, crypto or gift cards.
5. A fake "Airbnb" or "booking platform" that promises to hold your money.
6. Pressure: "many people want it, pay today".
7. Photos that also appear in other listings (run a reverse image search).
8. The landlord refuses to provide a Wohnungsgeberbestätigung, which means you can't register.

## Your rights as a tenant in Germany

- **Deposit:** at most **three months' cold rent**, payable in **three monthly instalments** (§ 551 BGB).
- **Rent control:** Berlin's Mietpreisbremse limits rent for most new contracts to about 10% above the local reference rent, with some exceptions.
- **Registration:** the landlord is legally required to confirm your move-in for Anmeldung (§ 19 Bundesmeldegesetz).

## FAQ

### When should I start looking for housing in Berlin?
Two to three months before arrival for private rooms. Apply for a Studierendenwerk dorm as soon as you're admitted, because waiting lists are long.

### How much does a student room cost in Berlin?
An average 20 m² room in a shared flat costs €659 per month in 2026 (MLP Student Housing Report). Dorm rooms are cheaper. Private student residences are usually more expensive.

### How do I avoid housing scams in Berlin?
Never pay before a viewing and a signed contract. Avoid untraceable payment methods. Be suspicious of landlords who are "abroad" and of prices far below market. Use verified platforms or partners.

### Can I register (Anmeldung) at a temporary or sublet room?
Yes, if the main tenant or landlord gives you a Wohnungsgeberbestätigung. Without it, you can't register at that address.
