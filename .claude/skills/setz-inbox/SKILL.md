# Inbox workflow

1. **Find** (Outlook, read only): inbox threads from the last 14 days where the last message is from someone
   else and needs an answer; and threads where he wrote last and has had no reply for 7 days or more. Skip
   automatic mail (calendar responses, booking confirmations, newsletters, notifications).
2. **Understand**: for each thread read the full conversation and the earlier exchange with that person or
   company; check `Setz/Settleezy/partners.md` for their stage and what was agreed.
3. **Decide and draft** (`inbox-manager`, using `Setz/Personal/writing-style.md`): choose the reply the way he
   does, write it in his voice, and save it with the Outlook reply-draft tool so it sits in the thread.
   Anything only he can decide goes in as `[[CHECK: …]]`.
4. **Organise follow-ups**: add a line per thread to `Setz/Log/followups.md` (who, what, next date). Where a date
   matters, add a calendar block for him alone. Meetings with other people are prepared, not sent.
5. **Report** a table: person and company, what they want, what the draft says, open `[[CHECK]]` points, next
   follow-up date. State clearly: every draft is PREPARED in Outlook Drafts; nothing was sent.

To send, he reviews in Outlook, or tells you exactly which drafts to send. Sending needs his approval per batch,
named by recipient.
