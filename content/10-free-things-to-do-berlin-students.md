# Free and Cheap Things to Do in Berlin for Students (2026)

*Last updated: October 2026 · By the Settleezy Team*

**Quick answer:** Berlin has many **always-free** places: the **East Side Gallery**, the **Berlin Wall Memorial** (Bernauer Straße), **Topographie des Terrors**, the **Memorial to the Murdered Jews of Europe**, the **Tränenpalast**, **Tempelhofer Feld**, and the **Reichstag dome** (free with advance registration). Under-30s can see opera and concerts for **€16–€18** with the **ClassicCard** (€28/year). Note: Berlin's free "Museum Sunday" programme was **discontinued at the end of 2024**.

## Always-free history and culture

1. **East Side Gallery**: 1.3 km of the Berlin Wall covered in murals
2. **Berlin Wall Memorial** (Gedenkstätte Berliner Mauer), Bernauer Straße
3. **Topographie des Terrors**: free exhibition on the Nazi regime
4. **Memorial to the Murdered Jews of Europe** and its information centre
5. **Tränenpalast**: the former border crossing at Friedrichstraße
6. **Museum in der Kulturbrauerei**: everyday life in the GDR
7. **Reichstag dome**: free, but register online in advance
8. **Brandenburg Gate, Gendarmenmarkt, Museum Island** (outside)
9. **Free walking tours**: tip-based, in English

## Free outdoors

10. **Tempelhofer Feld**: a former airport, now a huge park for cycling and barbecues
11. **Tiergarten** and the **Spree riverside**
12. **Mauerpark** on Sundays: flea market and open-air karaoke
13. **Lakes**: Schlachtensee, Krumme Lanke and Müggelsee are reachable with your semester ticket
14. **Teufelsberg** area and Grunewald forest

## Cheap culture with student discounts

15. **ClassicCard** (under 30): €28/year, then opera and ballet for €18 and concerts for €16 (last-minute: €13 and €11) at 10 venues including the Staatsoper, Deutsche Oper, Komische Oper, Konzerthaus and the Berliner Philharmoniker
16. **Student tickets (ermäßigt)** at almost all museums, theatres and cinemas: always show your student ID
17. **Cinema discount days**: many cinemas have cheaper tickets on specific weekdays
18. **University events**: free lectures, concerts, film screenings and parties

## Cheap everyday Berlin

19. **Mensa meals** for a few euros at Studierendenwerk canteens
20. **Public libraries (VÖBB)**: books, films, study spaces and digital media for a small annual fee
21. **Flea markets**: Mauerpark, Boxhagener Platz, Arkonaplatz
22. **Too Good To Go** and **foodsharing** for cheap or free food
23. **Day trips on your semester ticket**: Potsdam (Sanssouci park is free), Spreewald, Leipzig, the Baltic coast

## FAQ

### Are museums free in Berlin on Sundays?
Not any more. Berlin's "Museumssonntag" (free entry on the first Sunday of each month) was discontinued at the end of 2024. Several memorials and exhibitions are always free, and most museums give student discounts.

### What can students do in Berlin for free?
Visit the East Side Gallery, Berlin Wall Memorial, Topographie des Terrors, Tempelhofer Feld and the Reichstag dome (with registration), join tip-based walking tours, and go to free university events.

### How can students get cheap opera tickets in Berlin?
With the ClassicCard (for under-30s, €28/year): opera and ballet tickets cost €18 in advance or €13 last-minute.
